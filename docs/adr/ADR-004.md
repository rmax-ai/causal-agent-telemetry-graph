# ADR-004 — Edge Assertions Are Immutable Evidence

- **Status:** Accepted (SPEC.md §7)
- **Source:** SPEC.md §7 (ADR-004), §8 (Universal Edge Fields), §9 (`edge.asserted`), §10 (Causal-Linking Model), §21 (Testing Strategy); AGENTS.md (non-negotiable rule 2)

## Context

Every non-observed relation in CATG carries method, confidence, and evidence (SPEC.md §3). Edges are produced by a multi-stage linker with a defined precedence order (SPEC.md §10) and are the basis for policy decisions, detector evidence packages, and forensic explanation. If edge assertions could be silently rewritten (e.g., when the linker improves, or when a re-import changes linkage), then:

- past policy decisions and detector findings would no longer be reproducible (SPEC.md §21 — policy replay immutability is a required property-based test);
- the audit trail of "who asserted what, when, with which linker version" would be lost;
- old assertions could not remain auditable after a linkage change.

## Decision

**Edge assertions are immutable evidence: once asserted, they are never modified; old assertions must remain auditable** (SPEC.md §7 ADR-004).

- Every edge assertion is an event in the ledger (`edge.asserted`, SPEC.md §9) — the linker never writes edges directly to Kuzu (AGENTS.md "Graph Conventions").
- Every edge carries `evidenceIds`, `causalBasis`, `confidenceScore`, `linkerId`, and `linkerVersion` (SPEC.md §8), so the basis of an assertion is itself recorded.
- When linkage changes, the linker asserts **new** edges; superseded assertions remain in the ledger for audit (SPEC.md §7 — "Old assertions must remain auditable").
- Policy replay evaluates as-of a point in time (`--as-of-event`, SPEC.md §13) and must not mutate the original decision (SPEC.md §21).

## Consequences

### Positive

- Reproducibility: same ledger + same linker version ⇒ same edge set; replay is deterministic (SPEC.md §21).
- Auditability: every edge can be traced to its evidence IDs and linker version (SPEC.md §8).
- The graph remains a pure, rebuildable projection of the ledger (SPEC.md §7; ADR-001).

### Negative / Constraints

- Corrections are additive: a wrong assertion is superseded, not deleted, which grows the ledger (SPEC.md §9 immutable events).
- Linker changes require versioned linker identity (`linkerId`, `linkerVersion`) and re-assertion, not in-place upgrade (SPEC.md §8).

### Neutral

- Kuzu stores only the latest projection state; historical assertions live in the ledger (SPEC.md §11).
